# P3 — Luis Ortega

Fictional participant. Not a real person. Usability session, 15 September 2026. Task: decide whether to follow up on an open dispute.

**Role.** Bookkeeper. Works from a written checklist.

## Transcript

Moderator: It's been a few days. Do you need to do anything?

P3: I read the timeline. We opened it, you asked the merchant for a receipt, you're still waiting. That's a story. It isn't a date. I don't know if "still waiting" means I should call someone or sit down.

Moderator: What would make this easy to file away?

Moderator: Anything else you looked for?

P3: A way to tell the client, in their words, not ours. "In review" is our word. They'll ask me what it means, and I'll be guessing.
